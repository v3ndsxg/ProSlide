Third-party programs packaged in this app (Contents/Resources/Tools)
=====================================================================

ProSlide starts these as separate programs. Each is the official or
upstream-published build named in Scripts/tools.lock, checked against its
SHA-256 before it is packaged. They are not part of ProSlide's MIT-licensed code.

yt-dlp
  Fetches the video and audio. The source is released into the public domain
  (the Unlicense), but the downloadable program bundles code under the GNU
  GPL v3 or later, and the combined program is licensed that way.
  Source, license text and third-party notices: https://github.com/yt-dlp/yt-dlp

FFmpeg
  Merges, converts and extracts audio. This build, by Martin Riedl, is licensed
  under the GNU GPL v3 or later and includes libraries such as x264, x265 and
  LAME under their own licenses.
  Build and source information: https://ffmpeg.martin-riedl.de/
  FFmpeg itself: https://ffmpeg.org/

Deno
  A JavaScript runtime that yt-dlp needs to read YouTube pages. MIT License,
  Copyright the Deno authors.
  https://github.com/denoland/deno

Only download videos you own or have permission to use.
